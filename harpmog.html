ThemeAuto follows your phone or computer's light/dark setting.
Strings
VoicingSmooth keeps shared notes on the same strings when you change chord and moves the rest the shortest way. Spread rebuilds every chord from the bass up.
DensityHow many notes are on the strings. Sparse is clean for arpeggios; Full adds chord tones through the middle for big strums.
When you lift your fingerRing lets every string ring out. Hold to sustain works like an autoharp: notes ring while your finger stays down and are damped when you lift it. (Auto-strum and loops always ring.)
Export
Save loop as audioPlays your looper recording once more and saves it as a WAV file that loops seamlessly.
MIDI outSends every note to a synth or music app. Works in Chrome and Edge on computers and Android; iPhone and iPad Safari don't support MIDI.
